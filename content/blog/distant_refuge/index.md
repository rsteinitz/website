This [UC Santa Barbara feature](https://www.news.ucsb.edu/2020/019786/distant-refuge) follows my doctoral fieldwork in western Uganda and highlights the conservation potential of small, community-connected wildlife refuges. The piece focuses on Turaco Trails, a privately protected forest fragment that supports multiple monkey species while integrating conservation, ecotourism, and local community engagement.
