[This Mongabay article](https://news.mongabay.com/2020/01/answers-in-excrement-fecal-analysis-yields-insight-about-wild-primates/) explores how something as ordinary as poop can reveal hidden aspects of animal biology, including stress, reproduction, disease, diet, and social behavior, without ever needing to handle the animals themselves. The piece highlights the pioneering work of researchers like Karen Strier and Toni Ziegler in developing non-invasive primate endocrinology, and includes my own research in Uganda using urine and fecal biomarkers to study how ecological competition shapes the physiology of wild red-tailed monkeys.
