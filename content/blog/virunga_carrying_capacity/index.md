A [recent collaborative article in Gorilla Journal](https://www.berggorilla.org/fileadmin/user_upload/pdf/journal/journal_en/gorilla-journal-71-english.pdf) details how conservation success can create new ecological challenges. As mountain gorilla populations continue to recover in the Virunga Massif, questions surrounding habitat limits, human pressure, and long-term carrying capacity are becoming increasingly important for conservation planning and wildlife management.
