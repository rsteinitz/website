A photograph can hold a brief exchange: a chick reaching for food, an infant finding shelter, a youngster keeping contact as the group moves. Seen together, these moments show how much of early life unfolds close to a mother or other group members.

Across birds, primates, elephants, and lions, the gestures differ. These photographs follow a few of them without asking each frame to stand for an entire species. They are individual encounters, connected by the everyday work of raising young.
