## What can play teach us about growing up wild?

Play may look spontaneous, but it is also an important part of development. Through chasing, wrestling, climbing, and experimenting with their surroundings, young animals develop physical skills, test boundaries, and learn how to navigate their social worlds.

This essay brings together moments of play that reveal curiosity, confidence, and the gradual process of becoming independent.
